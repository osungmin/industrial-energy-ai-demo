# Chiller Maintenance & Energy Guide

## Condenser fouling
A rise in condenser approach temperature together with higher compressor power and reduced COP can indicate condenser-side fouling or degraded heat rejection. Inspect condenser tubes, cooling-water flow, cooling-tower condition, and sensor calibration before concluding root cause.

## Recommended checks
1. Compare current COP with a matched-load baseline.
2. Check condenser water inlet/outlet temperatures and flow.
3. Inspect condenser tubes and strainers.
4. Verify temperature and power sensors.
5. After maintenance, re-baseline performance.
